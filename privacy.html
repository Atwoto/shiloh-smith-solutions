Legal & Transparency

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you request a quotation or contact Shiloh & Smith Solutions via our website, email, or WhatsApp, we collect only the information necessary to evaluate your requirements and prepare an official proposal. This includes:

  • Full Name and Professional Title
  • Organization, Agency, Embassy, or Ministry name
  • Work Email Address
  • Telephone or WhatsApp contact number
  • Event details: dates, location/venue, estimated delegate count, language pairings, and equipment requirements
  • Any terms of reference or notes you include in your inquiry

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for legitimate business communications:

  • Preparing itemized quotations, proforma invoices, and equipment proposals
  • Confirming interpreter accreditation and scheduling technical personnel
  • Conducting technical venue site surveys and cross-border transport logistics
  • Communicating operational updates regarding your booked conference or meeting

We do NOT sell, rent, lease, or share your contact information with any third-party advertisers or marketing data brokers.

3. Diplomatic & Multilateral Confidentiality

Given the sensitive nature of multilateral assemblies, peace dialogues, bilateral negotiations, and judicial hearings, Shiloh & Smith Solutions enforces strict non-disclosure practices. Any event agendas, draft communiqués, or presentation documents shared with our team are kept under strict confidentiality and accessible only to assigned conference interpreters and lead technical operators bound by non-disclosure agreements (NDAs).

4. Data Retention & Security

Quotation requests and correspondence are stored securely in encrypted professional communication channels and retained only for institutional record-keeping, accounting compliance, and future service continuity. You may request the deletion of your contact history at any time by emailing us.
